# E-Commerce-Store-Data-Analysis-Using-Power-Bi
Developed an e-commerce sales dashboard to analyze sales, profit, quantity, and customer performance. Visualized monthly trends, regional sales, and product category insights using interactive charts and filters to support data-driven decision-making.

# Dataset Used 


## Questions ( KPIs) 

- What is the overall business performance in terms of Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value (AOV)?
- Which months generate the highest and lowest profits?
- Which states contribute the most to overall sales and profit?
- Which product category contributes the highest share of total quantity sold?
- Who are the top customers based on total sales amount?
- Which sub-categories generate the highest and lowest profits?
- How do sales and profit trends vary across different months and product categories?

## Process

- Imported and reviewed the e-commerce sales dataset to understand sales, profit, quantity, customer, state, category, and sub-category fields.
- Cleaned and organized the data to ensure consistent formats and accurate analysis.
- Created key performance indicators (KPIs), including Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value (AOV).
- Analyzed monthly profit trends to identify profitable and loss-making periods.
- Compared state-wise performance to understand regional contributions to business results.
- Evaluated category-wise quantity distribution and identified top customers based on sales.
- Analyzed sub-category profitability to identify products contributing the most and least profit.
- Designed an interactive dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and quarterly and state filters for easy business performance monitoring.

## Project Insights 

- The business generated approximately 438K in total sales, 37K in total profit, and 5,615 units in the quantity KPI, with an Average Order Value of approximately 121.
- Profit varies throughout the year, with May and December showing losses, while November and January record strong positive profits.
- Maharashtra contributes the highest profit among the displayed states, followed by Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Gujarat.
- Clothing dominates quantity sold, contributing 63%, followed by Electronics at 21% and Furniture at 17% (percentages may differ slightly due to rounding).
- Harivansh is the leading customer by sales amount among the customers displayed, followed by Madhav, Madan Mohan, and Shiva.
- Printers lead with approximately 8.6K in profit, followed by Bookcases at 6.5K. Tables record approximately 3.1K in profit among the displayed sub-categories.
- Investigating loss-making months and reviewing the profitability of underperforming products may help improve overall business performance.

## Conclusion 

- The Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard provides an overview of sales, profitability, customer contributions, product categories, and regional performance. The analysis highlights strong performing states, categories, and sub-categories while identifying loss making months that require further investigation. These insights can support better sales planning, product strategy, and data-driven business decisions.
